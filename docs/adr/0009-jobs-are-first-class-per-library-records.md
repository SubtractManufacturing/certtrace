# Jobs are first-class per-library records with historical assignments

Shops need to audit Materials and certs from a job (and see which jobs a piece relates to). A Job is a first-class Library-owned record keyed by a user-entered job number (unique per library after trim + case-fold), with a required shop-entered job date, optional job customer text (autocomplete from prior values — not a Customer entity), optional notes, and many-to-many historical Job assignments to Materials. Assignments are audit links only (no reservation/inventory); they may be added or removed from either side, including on archived Materials. v1 scope is per Library; cross-library jobs are deferred. Rejected: modeling job number as an identifier kind or ordinary field (no navigable Job or job→materials view), a first-class Customer module (filter-by-name is enough), and job status/lifecycle (jobs are listed and filtered; only Materials archive).
