# Material is one physical piece

CertTrace's unit of identity is a single physical piece (or remnant) of stock — not a heat/lot receipt, not a PO line. Lot, heat number, and purchase order hang off that piece as identifiers or fields. Search/filter answers “everything from heat X / PO Y” without a second entity type. Rejected: modeling the record as a lot receipt or PO line, which would break one-ID ↔ one label ↔ one storage location.
